THE PROBLEM

Engineering time gets lost between design and production.

Finished CAD is often only the beginning. Manufacturing teams still rely on repetitive checks, manual exports and company-specific rules before a part is ready to produce.

01

Manual preparation

Checking properties, material, thickness, naming and output folders consumes engineering time.

02

Inconsistent data

Different users and workflows can produce different naming, properties and production information.

03

Preventable mistakes

Every manual handoff creates another opportunity for missing information or incorrect files.

THE NAVOFLO APPROACH
