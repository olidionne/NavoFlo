# NavoFlo Privacy Policy — EN

**DRAFT-2026-08-20 — complete providers and retention periods**

Privacy Officer: **[[PRIVACY_OFFICER_TITLE]]**, **[[PRIVACY_EMAIL]]**.

## Information collected

Depending on features used, NavoFlo may collect name, email, organization,
account data, subscription/transaction identifiers, login/security logs, add-in
version, SOLIDWORKS version, and a minimized/pseudonymized device activation ID
where needed.

NavoFlo should not store full payment-card numbers; they are handled by
**[[PAYMENT_PROCESSOR]]**.

## CAD files

Under the current Navo2D/Navo3D architecture, opened files are processed locally
in the browser and are not uploaded to NavoFlo merely by opening them. Add-in
license calls should not send CAD content, geometry, filenames or paths.

If a future NavoAnalyzer feature requires cloud processing, NavoFlo must disclose
this before upload, specify purpose, subprocessors, region and retention, and
update this Policy before enabling the feature.

## Purposes

Personal information is used to create/secure accounts, deliver NavoBase/NavoPro
entitlements, bill customers, prevent fraud, provide support, maintain
compatibility and meet legal/accounting obligations.

## Cookies, analytics and profiling

Strictly necessary authentication/security cookies may be used. Non-essential
technology enabling identification, location or profiling must be disclosed and
enabled only with the required consent; privacy settings should be the most
protective by default.

legal anonymization requirements are met.
