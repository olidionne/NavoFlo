# Official reference sources used for the NavoFlo compliance draft

Reviewed: 2026-08-20. Verify again before launch because laws/guidance change.

## Québec consumer subscriptions / distance contracts

- Office de la protection du consommateur — Nouvelles protections pour les consommateurs:
  https://www.opc.gouv.qc.ca/consommateur/nouvelles-protections
- Office de la protection du consommateur — contrats conclus à distance:
  https://www.opc.gouv.qc.ca/commercant/pratique-commerce/contrats-distance/

Key launch date: 2026-09-12 for new rules including an easily found online
cancellation button for covered successive-performance subscriptions concluded
online where the consumer can cancel without cause.

## Québec privacy / Law 25

- Commission d'accès à l'information — principaux changements de la Loi 25:
  https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/sujets-et-domaines-dinteret/principaux-changements-loi-25

Includes privacy officer publication, governance requirements, privacy impact
assessments for information-system/electronic-service projects involving
personal information, assessments before communicating personal information
outside Québec, and privacy-by-default requirements.

## French language / adhesion contracts

- Office québécois de la langue française — Contrats d'adhésion:
  https://www.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/francisation/entreprises/Contrats-adhesion.pdf

Since 2023-06-01, a new adhesion contract must first be provided in French; an
other language may then be chosen expressly. A bilingual/simultaneous version is
possible if French is at least as prominent and transmitted on equal terms.
