Replace these before publishing or accepting payment:

- `[[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]]` — corporation/sole proprietor legal name.
- `[[BUSINESS_ADDRESS]]`
- `[[BUSINESS_PHONE]]`
- `[[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]`
- `[[PRIVACY_OFFICER_TITLE]]`
- `[[PRIVACY_EMAIL]]`
- `[[PAYMENT_PROCESSOR]]`
- `[[AUTH_PROVIDER]]`
- `[[HOSTING_PROVIDER]]`
- `[[EMAIL_PROVIDER]]`
- `[[DATA_REGIONS]]` — where account/support/billing logs are stored.
- `[[RETENTION_ACCOUNT]]`, `[[RETENTION_LOGS]]`, `[[RETENTION_SUPPORT]]`
- `[[JUDICIAL_DISTRICT]]` — for B2B clauses, subject to mandatory law.
- `[[CANCELLATION_URL]]`
- `[[OPEN_SOURCE_SOURCE_URL]]` — page hosting exact LGPL corresponding source /
  build instructions for the deployed occt-js/OCCT version.

Also decide and state at checkout:

- monthly/annual billing intervals;
- per-user / per-seat / per-device / concurrent rights;
- number of activations and offline grace period for add-ins;
- NavoBase price and included add-ins;
- NavoPro price and included services;
- whether trials/promotions exist;
- refund policy, always subject to mandatory consumer law;
- supported SOLIDWORKS versions;
- service-level/support commitments, if any.
