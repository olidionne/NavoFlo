NavoFlo Privacy Policy — EN

DRAFT-2026-08-20 — complete providers and retention periods

Privacy Officer: [[PRIVACY_OFFICER_TITLE]], [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]].

Information collected

Depending on features used, NavoFlo may collect name, email, organization, account data, subscription/transaction identifiers, login/security logs, add-in version, SOLIDWORKS version, and a minimized/pseudonymized device activation ID where needed.

NavoFlo should not store full payment-card numbers; they are handled by [[PAYMENT_PROCESSOR]].

CAD files

Under the current Navo2D/Navo3D architecture, opened files are processed locally in the browser and are not uploaded to NavoFlo merely by opening them. Add-in license calls should not send CAD content, geometry, filenames or paths.

If a future NavoAnalyzer feature requires cloud processing, NavoFlo must disclose this before upload, specify purpose, subprocessors, region and retention, and update this Policy before enabling the feature.

Purposes

Personal information is used to create/secure accounts, deliver NavoBase/NavoPro entitlements, bill customers, prevent fraud, provide support, maintain compatibility and meet legal/accounting obligations.

Providers

  • Payments: [[PAYMENT_PROCESSOR]]
  • Authentication: [[AUTH_PROVIDER]]
  • Hosting: [[HOSTING_PROVIDER]]
  • Email: [[EMAIL_PROVIDER]]
  • Processing/storage regions: [[DATA_REGIONS]]

Before personal information is communicated outside Québec, NavoFlo should complete the required privacy impact assessment and appropriate contractual safeguards.

Cookies, analytics and profiling

Strictly necessary authentication/security cookies may be used. Non-essential technology enabling identification, location or profiling must be disclosed and enabled only with the required consent; privacy settings should be the most protective by default.

Retention

  • Account: [[RETENTION_ACCOUNT]]
  • Technical/security logs: [[RETENTION_LOGS]]
  • Support: [[RETENTION_SUPPORT]]
  • Accounting: as required by law.

At end of retention, information is securely destroyed or anonymized only when legal anonymization requirements are met.

Rights and complaints

Requests for access, correction and other applicable rights may be sent to [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]]. Privacy complaints are handled by the Privacy Officer.

Incidents

NavoFlo maintains incident-response and confidentiality-incident records and provides legally required notices when the applicable risk threshold is met.

Changes

Material changes are published and communicated when legally required.
