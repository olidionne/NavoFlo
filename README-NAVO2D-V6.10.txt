NavoFlo / Navo2D V6.10 — Dimension arrow flip + commercial compliance pack
2026-08-20

Navo2D dimensions:
- AutoCAD-style individual arrow flipping.
- Selecting a generated dimension exposes blue arrowhead grips.
- Click arrow grip 1 or 2 to flip only that arrow in/out.
- Right-click dimension: Arrow fit Auto/In/Out, Flip arrow 1, Flip arrow 2,
  Reset arrow flips.
- Command AIDIMFLIPARROW / DIMFLIPARROW lets you pick the arrowhead to flip.
- Double-click dimension properties: independent arrow flip buttons plus global
  fit Auto/Inside/Outside.
- Linear, aligned, angular and diameter dimensions support two independent
  arrows where applicable; radius/leader dimensions support their single arrow.
- Filled arrowheads retained; Undo/Redo supported through history.
